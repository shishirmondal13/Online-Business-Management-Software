1 Mobile Phone Inventory & Dynamic Landed Costing
1.1. Dynamic Landed Purchase Price (Mobile Sector)
Stock Procurement
When purchasing batches of mobile devices, transport overheads are amortized equally across all units in the batch.
Allocated Transport = Total Transport Overhead / Batch Unit Quantity Net Purchase Price = Base Purchase Rate + Allocated Transport
double calculateNetPurchasePrice({required double purchaseRate, required double transportCost}) => _roundCurrency(purchaseRate + transportCost);
1.2. Realized Unit Profit on Mobile Sale
Unit Margin
Unit Profit = Selling Price - Net Purchase Price
double calculateMobileProfit({required double sellingPrice, required double netPurchasePrice}) => _roundCurrency(sellingPrice - netPurchasePrice);
2 Gadget & Accessory Multi-Cost Landed Margin Controller
2.1. Fully Burdened Gadget Landed Cost
Multi-Cost Allocation
Product Cost = Purchase Price + Transport + Packaging + Storage + Marketing + Damage Reserve + Other Costs
double calculateProductCost({required double purchasePrice, required double transportCost, required double packagingCost, required double storageCost, required double marketingCost, required double damageCost, required double otherCosts}) => _roundCurrency(purchasePrice + transportCost + packagingCost + storageCost + marketingCost + damageCost + otherCosts);
2.2. Markup Selling Price from Target Margin %
Pricing Strategy
Selling Price = Product Cost × (1 + (Target Profit Margin % / 100))
double calculateSellingPrice({required double productCost, required double profitMargin}) => _roundCurrency(productCost + (productCost * (profitMargin / 100)));
2.3. Weight-Based Transport Overhead Rate
Logistics Weight Pricing
Per-Gram Price = Per-Kg Logistics Rate / 1000 Unit Transport Cost = Per-Gram Price × Product Weight (Grams)
3 Point-of-Sale (POS), Split-Payments & Invoice Settlement
3.1. POS Cart Totals & Net Payable Invoice
Checkout Flow
Item Subtotal = Unit Selling Price × Quantity Invoice Subtotal = ∑(Item Subtotals) Grand Total (Net Payable) = Invoice Subtotal - Flat Discount
3.2. Multi-Payment Split, Customer Due ("Baki") & Change
Split Settlement
Total Paid = Cash Paid + Card Paid + MFS Paid IF Total Paid < Grand Total: Customer Due (Baki) = Grand Total - Total Paid Cashier Change = 0.00 ELSE: Customer Due (Baki) = 0.00 Cashier Change = Total Paid - Grand Total
3.3. Realized Gross Profit on POS Transaction
Sales Profit
Invoice Gross Profit = [ ∑(Item Selling Price - Item Net Purchase Cost) ] - Invoice Discount
4 Sales Returns, Refund Adjustments & Inventory Write-Offs
4.1. Sales Return Refund & Debt Reconciliation
Returns & Exchanges
Gross Refund Value = Unit Sale Price × Returned Quantity Due Reduction Amount = MIN(Gross Refund Value, Customer Outstanding Due Balance) Cash Refund Paid = Gross Refund Value - Due Reduction Amount
4.2. Damaged Inventory Write-Off Valuation
Shrinkage & Loss
Total Damage Loss = ∑(Damaged Handsets × Net Purchase Cost) + ∑(Damaged Gadget Qty × Unit Cost)
5 Customer Due ("Baki") Ledger Accounting
5.1. Customer Balance Update on Partial Collection
Receivables Ledger
Remaining Customer Due = Current Total Due - Installment Payment Received Total Market Receivables = ∑(All Customer Outstanding Due Balances)
6 Employee Payroll, Salary Advances & Deductions
6.1. Net Monthly Payable Salary
Payroll Processing
Net Payable Salary = (Base Salary + Previous Due Paid + Advance Repaid + Bonus) - Deductions
double get totalDeduction => (amount + duePaid + advance + bonus) - deductions;
7 Capital, Investment & Total Business Valuation
7.1. Available Liquid Capital
Cash Flow Balance
Available Liquid Capital = Initial Capital + Capital Recovered - Capital Invested
7.2. Total Enterprise Capital Valuation (Total Business Assets)
Balance Sheet Equity
Total Business Capital = Mobile Stock Value + Gadget Stock Value + Available Capital + Total Market Baki
double get totalCapital => mobileStockValue + gadgetStockValue + availableCapital + totalBaki;
8 Executive Financial Intelligence & Net Profit/Loss Engine
8.1. Consolidated True Net Profit Engine
P&L Master Engine
Realized Gross Profit = Total Sales Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) - Discounts Operating Expenses = ∑(Shop Expenses: Rent, Utility, Tea, Transport, Misc) + ∑(Salaries Paid) True Net Operating Profit = Gross Profit - Gross Loss - Operating Expenses - Damaged Stock Loss
double get netProfit => totalProfit - totalLoss - totalSalaryPaid - totalOtherExpenses;
8.2. Business Financial Health Ratios
Performance KPIs
Gross Profit Margin (%) = (Realized Gross Profit / Total Sales Revenue) × 100 Net Profit Margin (%) = (True Net Operating Profit / Total Sales Revenue) × 100 Profit Margin on Cost (%) = ((Selling Price - Net Landed Cost) / Net Landed Cost) × 100
